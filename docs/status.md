# SmartBot status

Last updated November 28, 2026.

## Current state

- Android adds **Translate** to the text selection menu in Books. It may appear under the menu’s three dots. Tapping it opens Google Translate with the selected text and the phone’s language as the initial target. You can change the target in Google Translate. The handoff uses Android’s standard text-processing action, which can also appear in other apps that support it.

- **Listen → Radio** streams franceinfo, France Inter, and France Culture directly from Radio France. Use **Add a station** for a direct HTTPS audio link, the trash icon to remove any station, and **Restore default stations** to bring presets back. Playback continues across SmartBot screens until you select the square stop icon or start other audio. The station list stays available offline. [Live radio](plans/podcast-listening.md#live-radio)

- The Four Leaves mark appears in the SmartBot header and follows the active light or dark theme. The browser tab and iOS Home Screen shortcut use the same mark, and Android uses it for adaptive and themed launcher icons.
- Press and hold a **Workspace** card, then drag it with the same touch to swap it. Drag any card immediately while swap mode is active. A translucent preview follows your finger or cursor, and both cards slide into place on release. With reduced motion enabled, the cards settle instantly. Tap outside the cards to finish editing. Use the small minus button to hide a card. **Settings → Workspace sections** restores hidden cards and opens the keyboard reorder controls. The order and visibility are saved on this device.
- **Settings → Appearance** applies a theme across SmartBot. Choose Grove, Ocean, or Clay and use System, Light, or Dark color mode. Changes apply immediately and are saved on this device for offline use. Existing installations start with Grove and follow the system color mode.
- **Today** shows recorded activity time, pages read, and listening time by activity and topic. The app tracks foreground Books and News use, source pages in newly converted PDFs, and podcast playback. Use **Log activity** for music, paper books, and other activities, or **Reading plan** to start or edit the routine. [Today activity report](plans/today-activity.md)
- **Ask SmartBot** opens from a movable chat bubble and closes when you tap outside the composer. Google Search is the default and opens results directly in your browser, including from the Android app. **Settings → Assistant** saves your provider choice. GPT and Gemini support typed questions and editable voice transcripts in the browser preview when their API keys are configured.
- **News** keeps its primary topic navigation on one row above the headlines. Swipe across the topic labels on narrow screens. The three-dot button stays visible and opens additional topics and **Saved interests**. **All** covers every available topic in the selected languages. Pull down at the top of News until the arrow flips, then release. A spinner appears while the feed updates. News opens ten matching headlines from free-to-read sources and appends ten more as you scroll. **For you** uses recent article opens on the device to surface related, unread stories. **Settings → News** saves English, French, and Vietnamese language choices and offers controls to turn personalization off or reset it. NASA releases open in SmartBot. The header star opens Saved, including any previously saved Canada.ca releases. Earlier News plan records remain in Progress.
- **Workspace** is the home screen. Its cards open Books, News, Listen, Markets, and previews for Languages and Movies. **Listen** remembers the last-used Music or Podcasts section. Music shows popular YouTube videos for France, Vietnam, and the United States, with song and artist search in the Listen tab row. Android opens matching YouTube Music results externally when the in-app catalog is unavailable. The player stays visible across screens. Music can also open your chosen service or play a saved Spotify playlist. Podcasts puts resume and followed shows first, with inline search in the Listen tab row and an **Add by RSS** option below the list. Followed shows, playback speed, and position are saved on the device. [YouTube music](plans/youtube-music.md) · [Podcast listening](plans/podcast-listening.md)
- The top bar has direct links to **Books** and **News**. The three-dot menu opens the other sections and Settings. Tap **SmartBot** to return home.
- Android opens **Music picks** after first-use approval without asking for a personal key. Live YouTube charts and catalog search for new installs are ready for a shared service deployment. [Android music service](plans/youtube-music.md#android-music-service)
- **Markets** opens **VN-Index** and **CAC 40** from the default watchlist. Search includes selected Euronext listings in Paris, Amsterdam, Brussels, and Lisbon, including **Valeo**, alongside U.S. stocks and ETFs, Vietnamese stocks, and USD crypto pairs. DNSE supplies VN-Index candlesticks in points. Euronext supplies closing-price lines with index levels in points and stock prices in EUR. Downloaded charts reopen offline. Eight U.S. equities and BTC/USD ship with daily candles through November 24, 2026. For crypto, Markets shows the latest Bitstamp trade and refreshes it every five minutes while visible and online. [Markets](../README.md#markets)
- Markets adds the **1D** and **4h** controls from `04eb46c`. The current daily series supplies the latest point for 1D and the latest four points for 4h. The web test suite, JavaScript syntax checks, and a chart render check for 1D, 4h, and 1W passed.
- Every Markets watchlist entry can be removed. Search can restore a removed default, and the watchlist can stay empty across app restarts.
- The first mobile-first web slice implements setup, an editable reading plan, the current book and page, completion and undo, and seven-day progress. A service worker caches the app after its initial load, and IndexedDB stores plans and reading progress on the device.
- **Books** puts the most recently opened book first in a compact list. Tap its row to resume at your saved place. The reader shows section progress and fits one page of text on screen. Use **Previous** and **Next** or swipe vertically near the left or right side of the page on a phone. Tap **+** beside **Books** to import a PDF or text file. In the library, use **⋯** beside a PDF to download it or its converted EPUB. Tap the page or use **Enter full view** to hide the reader toolbar. **Aa** adjusts text size, typeface, line spacing, and page color on this device.
- A debug APK bundles the same web app for Android 8.0 and newer. It loads its packaged files without a local server, and reading progress is stored in the app's WebView.
- Project-local Codex configuration defines five agent roles and two SmartBot workflows. The [design skill setup](codex-setup.md#design-skills) also installs pinned releases of Taste, Emil Kowalski's motion guidance, and Impeccable into the checkout.
- The Git workflow requires a task tracking record in each completed step's commit, an immediate GitHub push, and verification that the change and record reached `main`. Verify the source against live GitHub `main` before generating or refreshing a browser preview or building a preview APK. Parallel writing sessions each use their own branch and worktree or clone. [Git session workflow](codex-setup.md#session-workflow) · [Preview verification steps](codex-setup.md#verify-main-before-generating-a-preview) · [GitHub repository](https://github.com/trinamntn08/SmartBot)
- The repository owner grants standing approval for routine GitHub updates within requested SmartBot tasks, including publishing verified changes to `main`. [Agent instructions](../AGENTS.md#working-practices)
- The [personal workspace plan](plans/personal-workspace.md) expands the scope to in-app reading, GPT questions by text and voice, and news. The daily plan remains available as part of the workspace.
